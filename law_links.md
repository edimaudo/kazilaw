## Lawyer Information
* **Alberta:** Use the [Law Society of Alberta Lawyer Directory](https://lawsociety.ab.ca "Law Society of Alberta Directory") and filter your search parameters under practice area options. *(For an active group section, see the [CBA Alberta Labour & Employment Section](https://cba-alberta.org "CBA Alberta Labour Law")).*
* **British Columbia:** Access the [Law Society of British Columbia Lawyer Directory](https://lawsociety.bc.ca "Law Society of British Columbia Directory") to look up local active lawyers practicing in workplace fields.
* **Manitoba:** Filter by field through the public portal at the [Law Society of Manitoba Lawyer Lookup](https://lawsociety.mb.ca "Law Society of Manitoba Directory").
* **New Brunswick:** Search individual profiles on the [Law Society of New Brunswick Member Directory](https://lawsociety-barreau.nb.ca "Law Society of New Brunswick Directory").
* **Newfoundland and Labrador:** Find local legal professionals using the [Law Society of Newfoundland and Labrador Directory](https://lawsociety.nf.ca "Law Society of Newfoundland and Labrador Directory").
* **Nova Scotia:** Search by specific scope using the [Nova Scotia Barristers' Society Member Directory](https://nsbs.org "Nova Scotia Barristers' Society Directory").
* **Ontario:** Navigate directly to the [Law Society of Ontario Certified Specialists in Labour and Employment Law](https://lso.ca "LSO Certified Specialists") registry, or request a target connection through the [Law Society Referral Service](https://lso.ca "LSO Referral Service").
* **Prince Edward Island:** Browse the active practitioner lists via the [Law Society of Prince Edward Island Directory](https://lawsocietypei.ca "Law Society of PEI Directory").
* **Quebec:** Filter search inquiries for professional litigators using the [Barreau du Québec Bottin des avocats](https://barreau.qc.ca "Barreau du Québec Directory").
* **Saskatchewan:** Filter corporate or individual labor attorneys using the [Law Society of Saskatchewan Find a Lawyer](https://lawsociety.sk.ca "Law Society of Saskatchewan Directory") tool.
* **Northwest Territories:** Access active public records on the [Law Society of the Northwest Territories Directory](https://lawsociety.nt.ca "Law Society of NWT Directory").
* **Nunavut:** Find active regional practitioners listed on the [Law Society of Nunavut Member Directory](https://lawsociety.nu.ca "Law Society of Nunavut Directory").
* **Yukon:** Search active member listings directly on the [Law Society of Yukon Directory](https://lawsocietyyukon.com "Law Society of Yukon Directory").
