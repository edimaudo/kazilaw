Democratizing Employment law insights for the Canadian Workforce

The Problem

  • •Canadians often sign contracts containing non-compliant "enforceability" clauses.
  • •Most Legal reviews are unaffordable for individual employees (100+ CAD per hour).
  • •Manual review of employment contracts are time-prohibitive.

Technological Stack

  • •Web Framework & Server: FastAPI
  • •Agent & LLM Engine: Google GenAI SDK (google-genai) with Gemini 3.5 Flash
  • •Cloud Hosting & Compute: Google Cloud Run (Docker Containerized)
  • •Document Processing & OCR: PyMuPDF (fitz) and python-docx
  • •Template Engine: Jinja2

Key Features

  • •Compliance Reviews: Generates legal summary, enforceability status, applicable legal sections, next steps, email drafts.
  • •Employment Standard Q&A chatbot: Curious about Employment Standards? You can now ask questions via a chatbot.
